## About Page

### My Journey (Everett's real story — use verbatim or lightly edited)

I wasn't always confident in my body.

Growing up, I was bullied because of how I looked and struggled with anorexia, graduating high school at just 105 pounds.

I hated taking my shirt off and wore hoodies and long sleeves for years because I was embarrassed by my body. I felt weak, had little confidence, low energy, and didn't understand nutrition or how to improve my health. More than anything, I felt stuck and helpless.

Strength training changed everything.

Over the following years I gained more than 60 pounds, transformed my body, rebuilt my confidence, and discovered that fitness is about far more than appearance. It improves your energy, mindset, resilience, relationships, and quality of life.

That journey inspired me to earn degrees in Exercise Science and Sport & Exercise Psychology and dedicate my career to helping others experience the same transformation.

Today my mission is simple: Help people become stronger, healthier, more confident, and live longer by building habits that last for life.

> **Note:** This is authentic client-provided copy. The previous constraint saying Everett has no personal health-struggle origin story is removed. Use this story freely in Meet Everett sections on both the homepage and About page.
